Idea Book

Every Scoop and Idea, chapter by chapter — tick them off as you shoot. Red means it's missable.

Scoop counts are exact (53 total, cross‑referenced across multiple guides) — 5 points each. Idea counts are a location checklist, not a verified 1‑for‑1 match to the game's ~241‑entry catalogue: common objects like tables or lamps are listed per building for easy scanning, so the same catalogue idea may appear more than once here. Treat Ideas as "places worth pointing the camera," not gospel arithmetic toward 750.

The one real point of no return is entering the final boss floor at the end of Chapter 7 (Moon Flower Palace / Kazarov Stonehenge) — everything above assumes you're clearing chapters normally beforehand. Missed a one‑shot photo anyway? The in‑game Photo Album can import a photo from an older or different save file, so a save made before the trigger is a safety net.
